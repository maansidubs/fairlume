The problem

Most scoring models return a number and nothing else. When a regulator, an applicant, or your own risk team asks why, "the model said so" isn't an answer anyone can act on.

Fairlume is built the other way around: the explanation isn't a report generated after the fact. It's a byproduct of how the score is computed.

What you get

An instrument, not a black box.

Full attribution on every score

Each decision ships with a ranked, signed breakdown of the factors that produced it, computed directly from the model, not reverse-engineered after the fact.

Trained on real repayment outcomes

Built on historical lending data, not assumptions about what should matter.

Tuned to your risk appetite

Score bands and thresholds are configurable to your existing underwriting policy.

Drift and bias monitoring

Fairlume flags when a factor's influence shifts across populations, before it becomes a compliance problem.
